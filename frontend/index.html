How it works

Real: your pheromone blend lives on two real olfactory receptor neuron populations, ORN_DA1/ORN_DA2 (the actual cVA-pheromone glomerulus). That signal runs through the real male-cns:v1.0 connectome into real pC1/aSP courtship decision-hub neurons, which drive real DNp13 (courtship-acceptance) and DNa01 (steering) descending neurons -- DNp13 crossing a threshold is the actual MATCH/NOPE decision, not a dice roll.

The judged signal is your blend times the other fly's own hidden blend (their "receptiveness") -- so the same fly won't match everyone the same way, and who you swipe on matters.

Cosmetic (not real neurons): the trait names (Wing Shimmer, Leg Length, Antennae Curl, Buzz Confidence), the fly's color tint, and its name are flavor only. "Chemistry" is the one exception -- it's a real, coarse preview of your own fly's ceiling potential, deliberately banded so you can't read off an exact optimal blend.

The full connectome is ~176k real neurons; this game runs on a ~8.6k-neuron subgraph pruned down to the real courtship pathway (same real edges/weights, just fewer neurons far from that pathway) so it fits a free hosting tier's memory budget.
